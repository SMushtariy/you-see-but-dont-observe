What's in a session?

Every session follows the same four-part shape. You're not memorising facts — you're learning a way of seeing.

01

The era

One historical movement. What was happening in the world, what patrons wanted, why painters started painting that way.

02

The genre

One genre — portrait, landscape, still life. Its unwritten rules, its prestige, its hidden purpose.

03

Five micro-elements

Exactly five visual habits: a technique, a colour, a gesture. What they mean and why they work on the eye.

04

Masterpiece deep dive

One iconic painting — first impression, the hidden story, the technical magic, and why it still matters.

And at the end of every session — a 10-question mini-test. Not for grading. Just a way to lock in what you've seen.

About this project

You see, but don't observe — the line is borrowed from Sherlock Holmes, who said it to Watson about the steps of their own front door. This site is built on the same idea. Most of us look at paintings the way Watson looked at those stairs: we see shapes and colours, and we miss almost everything else.

Each session is written as a guided walk past one period, one genre, and one famous painting. Key terms are underlined in the text — tap them to jump to the glossary at the bottom of the page, then jump back to your spot in the reading. Hotspots on the master painting reveal short notes about specific details. Images are public-domain reproductions from Wikimedia Commons.

This is a personal learning project, not a course. There's no login, no tracking, no paywall. Your reading progress lives only in your own browser.
